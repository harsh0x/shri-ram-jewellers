Know Your Jewellery Guide

❖

Understanding what makes fine jewellery authentic and valuable before making your purchase.

The 6-Digit BIS Hallmark Unique Identification (HUID)

In India, mandatory hallmarking ensures you get the exact purity you pay for. A genuine hallmarked piece carries 3 laser marks:
1. BIS Triangular Logo: The national standard mark.
2. Purity Mark: 22K916 (91.6% pure gold) or 18K750 (75.0% pure gold).
3. 6-Digit Alphanumeric HUID: A unique tracking code registered on the Bureau of Indian Standards BIS Care mobile app.

💡 At Jai Sri Ram Jewellers: Every invoice lists your jewellery's specific HUID code so you can independently verify it on the BIS government portal anytime.
